What we teach

Three ways we get you licence-ready

Car lessons

One-to-one driving lessons

Structured lessons built around where you're weak and where you're already solid — no wasted time repeating what you've mastered. Every lesson ends with a short debrief so you know exactly what to work on before the next one.

  • Suitable from your first lesson through to advanced test manoeuvres
  • Roundabouts, hill starts, parallel parking and rural driving covered in depth
  • Progress notes after every lesson, shared with you or a parent/guardian on request

EDT

Essential Driver Training

If you're on a first learner permit for category B, the 12 EDT lessons are a legal requirement before you can sit your driving test. We deliver the full programme in a logical order, logged in your EDT log book as you go.

  • All 12 lessons delivered by a qualified ADI, in-car
  • Log book kept up to date and handed to you at the end of the programme
  • Can be booked as a block or spread out to suit your schedule

Test prep

Driving test preparation

An intensive run of lessons in the weeks before your test, focused on your test centre's routes and the manoeuvres examiners in your area tend to favour. We'll also do a mock test so nothing on the day feels unfamiliar.

  • Practice routes local to the Cork, Waterford and Clonmel test centres
  • Full mock test with examiner-style instructions
  • Nerves and technique both covered — test day strategy included

EDT programme

What the 12 lessons actually cover

Essential Driver Training is grouped into four phases. We move you through them in order, so each phase builds on the skills from the last one.

Exact lesson content follows the RSA's official EDT syllabus. Ask us for a copy of the full lesson-by-lesson breakdown.

01–02

Foundations

Vehicle checks, controls, cockpit drill, moving off and stopping, steering and observation.

03–06

Rural & urban roads

Junctions, roundabouts, hazard perception, and driving safely through towns and on country roads.

07–09

Night, weather & motorway

Reduced visibility, adverse conditions, and simulated motorway driving on a dual carriageway.

10–12

Independent driving & review

Following directions without step-by-step instruction, eco-safe driving, and a final progress review.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need to complete all 12 EDT lessons before my test?+

Yes — if this is your first Category B learner permit, all 12 EDT lessons must be completed and logged before you can book a driving test. If you held a permit before EDT rules applied to you, this may not apply; ask us to check your situation.

Can lessons be spread out or do they need to be back to back?+

Spread them out however suits you. Some learners do one a week, others complete the full 12 over an intensive fortnight before booking their test.

What car do I use for the driving test itself?+

You sit the test in your own car (it must meet the NDLS's roadworthiness and documentation requirements). All our lessons, including test prep, are in our dual-control car — the test itself is the one time you'll drive your own.

What areas do you actually cover?+

We're based centrally between the three counties and regularly teach in Cork city, Midleton, Fermoy, Waterford city, Dungarvan, Tramore, Clonmel, Carrick-on-Suir and Cahir. If your town isn't listed, get in touch — we likely still cover it.
